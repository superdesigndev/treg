What this step does, and why

What this step doesTurns two or three seed phrases into about forty related keywords with volume, cost per click and difficulty, labels each by search intent, keeps the ones close to a purchase, and pulls the full questions people type from “People also ask”.

Why it mattersPeople are not asking what you think they are. The questions nearest to buying are the ones worth winning in AI answers.

What this step doesSends the top questions to ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google’s AI Mode, then reads three things off every answer: the answer itself, the brands it names, and the websites it cites.

What this step does, and why

What this step doesScores every page AI cited for page and site strength, finds the sites that already link to your top competitors, and reads the best pages: who they list, when they were updated, and how to reach the people who run them.

Why it mattersThe easy wins are weak pages that AI quotes a lot. An opportunity score ranks every page, so you pitch the ten that matter.

What this step does, and why

What this step doesFor every source AI cites (review pages, Reddit threads, YouTube videos) it finds the person behind it and how to reach them, and drafts the pitch or reply. It also checks your own page: are AI bots allowed in, and do your first two sentences answer the questions.

Why it mattersAI repeats what these people publish. Get onto their pages and AI starts naming you. Nothing is sent for you: every draft is yours to review and send.
