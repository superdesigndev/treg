How do you make ads with Claude Code?

To make ads with Claude Code, you describe each job in a prompt and the agent does the work in a folder on your computer: it pulls your competitors' ads from the public ad libraries, finds the videos that spread in your category, reads the pages those ads sell from, writes a one-page brief, and makes image ads and a short video from your product photo. You approve each step. The ads go into your ad account paused, through Meta's own connector, and you switch them on. This playbook is the eleven prompts, in order, with what came back when we ran them.

New to this? Start at step 0. Already running ads? Pick your problem and jump in.

Step 4 · Teardown Run 27 Nov

How do I tell why a competitor’s ad works?

SymptomYou saved 200 ads and see no pattern.

You have. swipe-[today].csv from step 2.

type this
From the saved pull, take [brand]'s three oldest video ads on Meta. Remove duplicate copy first. For each: take a frame every three seconds and describe the first frame, the words on screen, the offer, who is on camera. Then list what repeats across all three. Save as teardown.md.

You get. teardown.md: three ads taken apart frame by frame, and what they share. Ours took 5 minutes and no treg charge. In all three ads the product was in the first frame, the brand was readable on the pack, and no price was shown.

Check.

  1. Say long-running, not winning. A start date does not show profit, and an old ad can be neglect.
  2. One long run means little. The same idea in several live versions, with the same offer, means more.
  3. It reads frames, not sound. Listen to the opening yourself.
NextNote the format and the offer. Step 5 checks the offer.
treg doesSupplies the media link for each ad. Claude Code reads the frames on your machine.
You decideWhat to borrow. Take the structure. Never rebuild their ad with your logo: the library lets you look, and gives you no right to the creative, the actor or the music.

Step 5 · Offer Run 27 Nov · under $0.01

What is the offer behind the ad?

SymptomYou copy the ad and miss the price, the bundle and the shipping line that make it convert.

You have. swipe-[today].csv from step 2.

type this
For each brand in the saved pull, find the page most of its ads link to. Read it. Give price, pack size, subscription offer, shipping line. If a link is dead, say which page you read instead. Then list three gaps [my brand] could use. Save as offers.md.

You get. offers.md: a price and offer table for each competitor, and three gaps. Ours read three pages in 5 minutes, for under a cent. Per stick, the three brands charged $1.20 to $1.56 for a single order and $0.99 to $1.30 on subscription.

Check.

  1. Sale weeks. Ours ran in Black Friday week, with a 30% code live on one site.
  2. Blocked pages. One site refused to load; Claude said so and named the page it read instead.
  3. Compare like with like: price per unit, on the pack size most ads link to.
NextYou now have hooks.md, teardown.md and offers.md. That is the research.
treg doesReads a web page for a fraction of a cent. A dead link is free.
You decideWhich gap is true for your product.

Step 6 · Brief Run 27 Nov · $0

How do I write a creative brief with Claude Code?

SymptomYou go from a folder of competitor ads straight to making things, and every ad says three things at once.

You have. product.md, offers.md, hooks.md.

type this
Read product.md, offers.md and hooks.md. Write brief.md, one page: who the ad is for and the problem it solves, then three angles. For each angle: the hook pattern it borrows from hooks.md, the format (static or video), the opening line, and the one claim it makes. Use only facts in product.md. No health claims, no invented reviews or customers. Mark the angle to test first and say why. Then stop.

Step 9 · Launch Method

How do I connect Claude Code to my Meta ad account safely?

SymptomYou are afraid an agent will spend money, change a budget or get the account restricted.

You have. Your ads, and a Meta ad account you can log in to.

Set up once. Connect Meta’s own ads connector. It is made by Meta and free. Search “Meta ads AI connectors” in Meta’s Business Help Centre and follow it. It is an open beta, and not every account has every tool yet.

type this
Using the Meta ads connector, read my account first and change nothing. Show me my campaigns and what each spent in the last 7 days. Then write a plan: one new campaign for testing, one ad set, my five image ads and one video, [$X] a day. Show me the plan. When I approve, create everything paused. Turn nothing on.

You get. A plan, then a paused campaign you can open in Ads Manager. We have not run this step ourselves; it follows Meta’s documentation and the open-source ads skills.

Check.

  1. In Ads Manager, before you switch it on: the Page, the link, the budget, the country, each ad’s text.
  2. New ads sit in their own ad set. Next to an old winner they get no spend.
  3. In Meta Business settings, block the actions you do not want and cap the budget the connector can set.

Google. Google’s own connector reads your account. It cannot create or change campaigns, ads, budgets or bids, so upload there by hand.

Is it safe? Meta’s help page says using its connector alone does not put an account at risk. A few advertisers report restrictions after connecting an agent, and none has shown the cause. Start read-only for a week.

Step 10 · Keep or kill Method

Which ads do I keep and which do I kill?

SymptomMeta puts most of the budget on one ad, results swing day to day, and you kill ads on a feeling.

You have. Ads that have run for at least four days.

type this
Using the Meta ads connector, read only. For each ad in the test campaign over the last 7 days: spend, purchases, cost per purchase, click rate. My target cost per purchase is [$X]. Sort into: keep, kill, too early. "Too early" means it has spent less than twice my target. If tracking looks broken, stop and tell me. Change nothing.

You get. Three lists: keep, kill, too early. We have not run this step ourselves.

Check.

  1. Tracking first. If purchases are zero everywhere, the ads are not the problem.
  2. Most of the budget goes to one ad. The rest are too early, not losers.
  3. “Twice your target, and not before four days” is what media buyers do. It is not a Meta rule, and they disagree on the number.
NextPause the kills yourself. Move a keeper to its own ad set. Write one line in results.md: which angle won.
treg doesNothing here. Your results come from your own account.
You decideThe target cost, and every pause.

Appendix D

Glossary and questions

Ad libraryThe public page where a platform shows the ads running on it. Meta, Google, TikTok and LinkedIn each have one.
Swipe fileYour saved examples of other people’s ads (step 2).
HookThe opening line or first second of an ad (step 3).
AngleOne person, one problem, one claim (step 6).
BriefOne page that says who the ad is for, the angle, the opening line and the format (step 6).

Ad setThe group inside a campaign that holds ads and a budget (step 9).
Cost per purchaseSpend divided by purchases. The number step 10 sorts on.
Can Claude Code see my competitors' ads?

Yes, the public ones. Meta, Google, TikTok and LinkedIn each publish an ad library. Claude Code can read them through a data tool such as treg.to and save the ads to a file. It sees the creative, the copy, the start date and the landing page. It does not see spend or results: no library publishes those for commercial ads.

Does a long-running ad mean it is winning?

Not on its own. A start date shows the ad was not turned off. It does not show profit, and an old ad can be neglect. The stronger sign is the same idea running in several live versions, with the same offer and landing page, still there when you check again a week later.

Is it safe to connect Claude to my Meta ad account?

Meta's help page says using its own ads connector does not by itself put an account at risk; restrictions follow policy violations, payment problems and compromised accounts. A few advertisers report restrictions after connecting an agent, and none has shown the cause. Start read-only, create everything paused, and set the connector's action and budget limits in Meta Business settings.

How much does it cost to run this playbook?

In our 27 Nov 2026 run the research steps (1 to 6) cost $0.18 on treg.to, the image ads were free because Claude Code laid out our own product photo, and one 8-second video cost $2.19. Claude Code's own usage and your ad spend are separate.

Do AI-made ads perform as well as real ones?

We have not measured it, and no source we read gives a reliable answer. Advertisers who posted their own tests describe AI ads as a cheap way to find an angle, with a filmed remake of the winner lasting longer. Treat the ads from steps 7 and 8 as tests.
