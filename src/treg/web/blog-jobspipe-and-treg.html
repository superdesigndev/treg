JobsPipe and treg: hiring signals, people search and job data in one agent

JobsPipe knows who is hiring, for what and at what pay. treg adds the people, the company profile and the other signals. Here is what sales teams, recruiters and job seekers do with the two together, with the prompts and what each run costs.

JobsPipe knows who is hiring, for which role, where, and at what pay. It reads 30+ job boards and applicant tracking systems and returns every posting in one schema. It does not know the people: who runs the team behind a job post, who would be a strong candidate for it, or how to reach either of them.

treg does. JobsPipe is in the treg catalog, so an agent that starts from a job post can go on to search for people, enrich a company and check an email with the same token.

Three kinds of teams use the two together. Each section below has the prompt, the calls the agent makes and what the run costs.

Sales and GTM: signals that say a company is ready to buy

A job post is a company saying what it is about to spend on. Five new data engineer roles mean a budget, a deadline and a problem that is not solved yet. JobsPipe turns that into a ranked list of accounts and adds the technologies each one names in its postings. treg adds the company profile, funding and launch news, and the person who owns the team.

  • Hiring intent. Rank accounts by how many roles they opened for the team that buys your product.
  • Stacked signals. Hiring plus a funding round plus a named tool is a stronger reason to call than any one of them.
  • The buyer, not the recruiter. Search for the head of the team by title and get a checked work email.

Recruiters: reqs to win and people to place

An agency needs two things: a client with an open role, and a person to put forward for it. JobsPipe supplies the first, the morning a role is posted. treg supplies the second, with a people search that takes a title, a skill and a city, and it also finds the hiring manager so the pitch goes to the person who feels the gap.

  • New business. See which employers in your niche opened roles this week and who manages each team.
  • Candidate sourcing. Describe the req in plain words and get people who match it, with their current role.
  • Both sides reached. Emails for the manager and the candidates, checked before anything is sent.

Job seekers: the role, the manager and the pay

Applying through the form is the slow lane. An agent with both tools finds the roles worth your time, tells you what the market pays for the title, and names the manager who owns each role so you can write to a person.

  • Every board at once. One search across 30+ sources, filtered by pay, remote and seniority.
  • Know your number. Posted pay percentiles for the title and country before you name a figure.
  • Skip the queue. The hiring manager and a checked email for the roles you care about most.

Through treg, or direct?

Use JobsPipe through treg when an agent is doing the work and job data is one step in a longer chain. Go to the JobsPipe API directly when you are building a product on the data: webhooks, bulk pulls and a monthly plan live there, with a free tier. Plenty of teams do both, and a JobsPipe key registered in treg is used ahead of treg's and never metered.
