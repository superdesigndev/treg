About these stats {{insightWindow}}

Verdict rate is the share of unique evaluable requests that returned a verdict, including valid, invalid and unknown answers. It does not measure verification accuracy.

Hit rate is the share of unique evaluable lookups that returned the task’s required fields. We keep the latest evaluable response per identical request for each vendor, so repeat calls do not dominate. Under 20 unique lookups shows no rate; under 100 is a small sample.

Known bad requests, rate limits, balance/credit failures, access and service failures, refusals and pending responses are excluded. Genuine no-result responses count as misses; ambiguous or missing evidence is omitted. Response time is the median of successful calls, including repeats, with at least 20 returned results.

{{verifiedExplanation}} Verifier details and check dates are available on hover. Samples with fewer than 20 completed checks are withheld. Risky, unknown and conflicting judgments are not counted as valid; unfinished checks are excluded.
