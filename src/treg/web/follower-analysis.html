How it works.How treg picks, reads and sorts your followers.

  1. 1. Read your followers

    Pages from across your whole follower list, old follows and new, minus empty accounts. 100 are picked at random.

    picked at randomnot pickedempty, left out

  2. 2. Read their bios

    One profile read each. The follower list carries their photo, so faces cost nothing extra.

    ✓
    bio read
    no bio, no badge: a miss

  3. 3. Jev sorts each one

    Jev, a small decision model, puts each follower in one of your groups, or Other, and reads how senior they are.

    bio→ Jev→FounderEngineerOther

  4. 4. Unclear? Read their posts

    When Jev is unsure or picks Other, treg adds their latest posts and Jev decides again. Followers with no bio and no posts are left out.

    unclear bio+latest posts→ Jev decides again
