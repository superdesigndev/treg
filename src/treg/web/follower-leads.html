How it works.The money goes where the evidence is thin, and nowhere else.

  1. 1

    Read your followers

    The newest first, with each one's name, follower count and company badge. Empty accounts are skipped for free.

  2. 2

    Read their bios

    One profile read per follower. Followers with no bio and no badge count as misses.

  3. 3

    Jev judges each one

    treg works out what you sell from your own profile, website and posts. Jev, a small decision model, then scores every follower against it: 0 not a buyer, 1 would use it, 2 picks and pays. A lead scores 1.5 or more.

  4. 4

    Enrich, then Jev decides again

    When a bio is too thin to judge, treg enriches it with the person's latest posts and the company behind their website. Jev then decides again on the fuller picture. No email, phone or LinkedIn lookups.
