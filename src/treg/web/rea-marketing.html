Copy these plays

01

New models on social, old models on sale

Product linesEffortA week

DJI’s brand account pushes the Pocket 4P (2.7M views) while Black Friday creator videos sell last year’s Pocket 3.

Start3 weeks before Black FridayWatchOld-model Black Friday sales vs whether new-model prices held
Prompt

Using treg, list the products we launched in the last 12 months and the older models they replace, with each one’s current price on our store and Amazon. Recommend which older models take the Black Friday discount and which new ones stay at full price, and draft 3 brand posts for the new ones with no price in them.

02

Flood TikTok Shop with affiliates

CollabsEffortTwo weeks

135 of DJI’s 173 real collab posts carry a TikTok Shop cart; 28 are paid partnerships.

Start3 weeks before Black FridayWatchAffiliate video count and GMV
Prompt

Using treg, search TikTok for creators who posted TikTok Shop videos about [my category] in the last 3 months. Keep US accounts with 10k-500k followers, list the 30 with the highest views per follower (handle, followers, top video link, views), and draft a one-paragraph affiliate invite for each tier.

03

Collect emails with early prices

OwnedEffortA day

Before Black Friday DJI pushes “subscribe for an extra 5% off + preview Black Friday prices”, with model names in its ad keywords.

Start2-3 weeks before Black FridayWatchNew subscribers and open rate during Black Friday
Prompt

Using treg, pull this month’s US search volume for each of [my top 10 products] + “black friday”. Pick the 10 keywords to bid on, and write the signup offer for them: one line for the site banner and one for the search ad, an extra discount for an email plus early access to Black Friday prices.

04

Run creator clips as Meta ads

PaidEffortA week

DJI’s Meta ads are creator clips credited to the creator, with no price; two run from creator pages as partnership ads.

Start4 weeks before Black FridayWatchCost per view and saves on creator-clip ads vs brand-made ads
Prompt

Using treg, search TikTok and Instagram for videos that show [my product] in use, posted in the last 6 months. List the 10 with the most views (creator, link, views, hook in the first 3 seconds), then write ad copy for each in this shape: one line on what the shot is, a question, the creator credit, 3 hashtags. No price.

05

Answer the dupe searches

Search · SocialEffortA week

15 collab posts sell a “DJI dupe” (1.28M views); other brands sold about 105K units in Pocket 3 searches on TikTok Shop vs 2.5K for DJI.

Start2 weeks before Black FridayWatchShare of “[brand] dupe” search results that are ours
Prompt

Using treg, search TikTok, TikTok Shop and Google Shopping for “[my brand] dupe”. List the top 10 results on each (title, seller, price, units sold or views), work out what share of units go to dupes, and outline 3 short videos that show what the real product does that the top dupe can’t.

06

Put the Black Friday price where buyers finish

RetailEffortA day

The Pocket 3 is $439 at DJI’s store, Amazon and Best Buy, but $359-389 from marketplace sellers on Walmart and TikTok Shop.

Start1 week before Black FridayWatchAmazon units and rank during Black Friday week
Prompt

Using treg, pull today’s price for [my top 5 products] on our own store, Amazon, Walmart, Best Buy and TikTok Shop, with the seller name for each listing. Show a table of price by seller, flag every seller below our own price, and repeat it daily until Black Friday + 7.

3 things to skip, and why
  • Promo posts on the brand accountDJI’s own TikTok posts no promos; the price is left to affiliates and ads.
  • Sitewide discountsTen days out DJI’s store discounts 3 of 26 variants; the Pocket 3 is at list price.
  • Starting on Black Friday itselfLast year search rose 11 days before Black Friday; starting on the day hands that demand to rivals.

Counts of ads, posts and moves are everything found, never scaled. Spend and sales are estimates at market rates, with a range. Snapshots Nov 16-17, 2026. treg is not affiliated with DJI.
