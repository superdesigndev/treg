    In Claude Code, Codex or any agent with treg: post version B to my Instagram and TikTok at 6pm.

    It posts through treg

    Your agent shows you the post, waits for your yes, posts it, then reads back views, likes and comments.

Prompt

Ask me which of my accounts connected in treg to post to, when, and for the two versions to test, A and B, with a public link to any image or video. Show me both before anything goes out, and post only after I say yes. Post through treg where the platform allows; where it only takes a draft (TikTok's inbox), send the draft and tell me to finish it in the app. Then read back views, likes, comments and shares from my accounts at 1h, 6h, 24h and 48h, side by side.
